How to read this

311 vs seasonal norm compares the last 28 days of each complaint type with the same weeks in the prior three years (adjusted for citywide volume) and with the recent eight-week pace; the "expected" figure is the higher of the two. Other record systems compare the latest window with the window immediately before it — HPD violations and DOB complaints (30 days), HPD complaints (28 days), residential marshal evictions and HPD vacate orders (60 days). Buildings are tax lots where at least two independent families of records agree (resident reports; inspector findings; enforcement or court action; state licensing). Leads are the strongest of these, checked against Google News and local RSS for the last six weeks; anything already covered is marked.

Full method, thresholds, dead feeds and failure modes: leads-methodology.md. Data: leads.json · this week's brief · source.

Read with care

These are signals, not findings. A building with 40 calls in two weeks may have one persistent caller; a complaint type above its norm may reflect a change in how 311 files things; a "new" descriptor may be a renamed old one. Every number links to the exact public query that produced it.
